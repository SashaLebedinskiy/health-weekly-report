Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026.

Health Weekly Report is a personal, non-commercial integration used only by its owner. It accesses Oura data only after the owner grants OAuth permission.

Data used

The integration may process daily sleep and readiness summaries, activity, workouts, guided or unguided sessions, heart-rate-related summaries, stress, blood-oxygen and heart-health metrics selected by the owner.

Purpose and storage

Data is used solely to prepare a private weekly wellbeing report. Detailed records and OAuth credentials are stored locally by the owner. The report is delivered to the owner's private Telegram chat.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

Control

The owner can revoke Oura access at any time in Oura or delete local records and the Telegram bot configuration.

Contact

Contact the owner through the GitHub profile.
